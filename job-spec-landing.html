Frequently asked questions

Is the job spec generator free?

Yes — it's completely free. Answer a few questions about the role and you'll receive a tailored job specification and person specification you can edit and download.

What's the difference between a job specification and a person specification?

A job specification describes the role — its purpose, responsibilities and the experience and skills required. A person specification describes the ideal candidate's behaviours and personality, so you hire someone who will genuinely thrive in the role.

How long does it take?

A few minutes to answer the questions, then around 30 seconds to generate your spec.
